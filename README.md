### ✨ About

Minimalist Habit Tracker is a clean, aesthetic app designed to keep you focused on your goals without any clutter. It is 100% offline, completely ad-free, and features a detailed yearly overview so you can easily see your consistency at a glance.


<br>

### 🚀 Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 🎨 **Aesthetic UI** | A clean, minimal design focused on keeping things simple. |
| 📅 **Yearly Overview** | See your full year of progress and habit hits at a glance. |
| 📱 **100% Offline** | All your data is stored locally on your device for total privacy. |
| 🚫 **Zero Ads** | Completely ad-free experience with no distractions. |
